Consistency across digital touchpoints is not optional — it is the foundation of trust. Every pixel, post, and email that carries the Alchemetryx name is an opportunity to reinforce who you are: bold, data-driven, and genuinely transformative. Whether someone encounters you on a landing page, in their LinkedIn feed, or inside a proposal deck, the experience should feel unmistakably Alchemetryx — warm in tone, sharp in execution, and rooted in the brand's visual identity.

## Website & Landing Pages

Your website is the single most important digital asset in the Alchemetryx ecosystem. It must project clarity and confidence while inviting exploration. Apply the following rules to every page you build or commission.

**Backgrounds and Layout**

Use Off-White (`#f9f5f0`) as the default section background throughout the site. This creates a warm, premium feel that distinguishes Alchemetryx from the sea of pure-white corporate websites. Reserve Deep Navy (`#1a1f36`) exclusively for footers and dark-section moments — such as testimonial blocks or hero overlays — where contrast and gravitas are needed.

**Button Styles**

Two button styles are permitted on the website:

- **Primary CTA:** Orange (`#ef7025`) fill with white text. Use for the single most important action on the page — a booking link, a contact form, a download.
- **Secondary CTA:** Teal (`#4fa1ab`) outline with Teal text on a transparent background. Use for supporting actions — learn more links, secondary navigation prompts.

Never invert these roles. Orange is reserved for urgency and action; Teal signals exploration and depth.

**Spacing**

Generous whitespace is a brand value, not a design afterthought. Apply a minimum of 48px vertical padding to every section. On desktop breakpoints, aim for 80–100px between major content blocks. Cramped layouts contradict the Alchemetryx ethos of clarity.

**Imagery**

Follow the full imagery guidelines for all web visuals. As a rule of thumb: no generic stock photography, no posed corporate handshakes, and no overly filtered lifestyle imagery. Prefer authentic photography, data-driven illustrations, and abstract visuals that evoke transformation.

## Social Media

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="LinkedIn">
    LinkedIn is Alchemetryx's primary professional stage. Use it for thought leadership, case study highlights, strategic perspectives, and culture content that signals who you are to prospective clients and talent alike.

    **Tone:** Professional, considered, and confident. Write as a practitioner who has seen the data and formed a clear view — not as a brand account reciting talking points.

    **Content types:** Long-form articles, insight carousels, project spotlights, team introductions, and industry commentary. Prioritise posts that invite conversation over posts that simply announce.

    **Visual specs:**
    - Profile banner: 1584×396px — use brand colour backgrounds (Orange or Teal) with the Alchemetryx wordmark
    - Shared image / link preview: 1200×627px
    - Profile photo: Alchemetryx logo on white or Off-White background — never on a busy photographic background
    - Carousel slides: 1080×1080px, consistent slide template using brand fonts and colours

    **Posting cadence:** Aim for three to four posts per week. Mix long-form with short punchy insights. Always include a clear point of view — vague content underperforms on LinkedIn.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Instagram">
    Instagram is a visual-first channel. Use it to communicate the aesthetic energy, culture, and creative rigour of Alchemetryx. Every post should be immediately recognisable as part of the same visual system.

    **Tone:** Warm, creative, and human. You can be slightly more playful here than on LinkedIn, but never sacrifice substance for style.

    **Content types:** Quote graphics, behind-the-scenes content, team culture moments, visual data stories, process and methodology snippets, and brand milestone celebrations.

    **Visual specs:**
    - Standard post: 1080×1080px square
    - Portrait post: 1080×1350px
    - Stories: 1080×1920px with Alchemetryx branding in the top-left corner
    - Reels cover image: 1080×1920px

    **Visual treatment:** Apply a consistent colour-grading treatment to all photography — warm tones, slightly lifted shadows, and reduced contrast to complement the brand palette. Quote graphics must use brand colour backgrounds (Orange, Peach, or Teal) with Inter or Playfair Display typography. Never use clashing neon or desaturated greyscale backgrounds.

    **Grid discipline:** Plan your grid in advance. Alternate between photo content and brand-colour graphic tiles so the grid never feels monotonous.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Twitter / X">
    Twitter/X is where Alchemetryx sharpens its point of view in public. Use it for concise, opinionated takes on data strategy, business transformation, and the consulting industry. This is the channel where voice matters most.

    **Tone:** Sharp, direct, and confident. Avoid hedging language. If Alchemetryx has a view, state it clearly.

    **Content types:** Single punchy observations (under 240 characters), threads for longer ideas, reactions to industry news, and reposts of team or client content.

    **Thread format:** When an idea deserves more than a single tweet, use a numbered thread. Lead with the most arresting statement — do not bury the headline. Each thread should end with a clear takeaway or call to action.

    **Visual content:** Keep image use purposeful. A well-formatted quote graphic, a data chart, or a brand-colour tile adds more value than a stock image. Maintain the same visual standards as other channels — 1200×675px landscape for shared images.

    **Engagement:** Respond to replies and mentions promptly. Twitter/X rewards active participation, not broadcast-only behaviour.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Email

Email is one of the highest-trust channels you have — someone has given you access to their inbox. Honour that with design and copy that is clear, valuable, and visually on-brand.

**Template Structure**

Every Alchemetryx email template follows this layout:

- **Header:** A full-width bar in either Orange (`#ef7025`) or Teal (`#4fa1ab`), containing the Alchemetryx wordmark in white. Use Orange for campaign and promotional emails; use Teal for informational and nurture sequences.
- **Body:** White background (`#ffffff`). Maintain a maximum content width of 600px. Left-align body text — never justify.
- **Footer:** Deep Navy (`#1a1f36`) background with white body text. Include the physical address, unsubscribe link, and social icons.

**Typography**

- Body text: Inter, 16px, regular weight, Dark Charcoal (`#2d2d2d`) — not pure black
- H2 headings: Inter, 22px, semi-bold
- H3 subheadings: Inter, 18px, semi-bold
- Pull quotes or callout text: Playfair Display, italic

**CTA Buttons**

Use a single primary CTA per email. Button style: Orange (`#ef7025`) fill, white text, Inter semi-bold 16px, 12px vertical padding, 24px horizontal padding. Rounded corners (4px radius) are preferred for a modern but not over-designed feel.

<Note>
  Never use image-only emails. Always include a plain-text fallback and ensure all images have descriptive alt text. Many email clients block images by default — your message should make sense without them.
</Note>

## Presentations

Presentations are high-stakes brand moments. A poorly branded deck damages credibility before the first word is spoken. Every Alchemetryx presentation should feel like a coherent, professional argument — not a collection of slides.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the Alchemetryx Slide Template">
    Always begin with the official Alchemetryx PowerPoint or Google Slides template. Do not start from a blank canvas or adapt a client's template unless explicitly agreed. The template includes the correct fonts (Inter and Playfair Display), brand colours, and pre-built slide layouts for title slides, section dividers, data slides, and closing slides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lead With the Mission Statement Slide">
    The second slide — immediately after the title — must always be the Alchemetryx mission statement slide. This grounds the audience in the Alchemetryx perspective and signals that the work they are about to see is part of a larger, purposeful system. It also reinforces brand recognition in client-facing settings where the deck may be shared beyond the room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Teal for Data Visualisations">
    All charts, graphs, tables, and data visualisations must use Teal (`#4fa1ab`) as the primary data colour. Use Peach (`#eec9bc`) or a lighter Teal tint as secondary series colours. Avoid using Orange in data charts — it is reserved for calls to action and accent moments, not data encoding. Ensure every chart has a clear title, labelled axes, and a concise interpretive caption directly beneath it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close With a Transformation Call-to-Action Slide">
    The final content slide of every deck must be a closing CTA slide. Use a Deep Navy background, the Alchemetryx wordmark in white or Orange, a single bold statement that encapsulates the transformation on offer, and the relevant contact details. Do not end a deck on a Q&A or blank slide — always close with intention and a clear next step for the audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Digital Advertising

Digital advertising extends your brand to audiences who may not yet know Alchemetryx. These executions must be held to the same visual standards as every other brand touchpoint — a low-quality banner ad is worse than no banner ad.

**Core Rules for All Ad Formats**

Every digital advertisement produced for Alchemetryx must include:

1. The Alchemetryx logo (wordmark or symbol, depending on space)
2. A clear, concise tagline or value statement
3. A single, prominent call-to-action — one CTA per ad, no exceptions

Background colours must be drawn exclusively from the brand palette. Do not introduce new colours, gradients not derived from the brand palette, or photography-as-background unless it meets the full imagery guidelines.

**Photography in Ads**

Avoid stock photography in display advertising. When photography is used, it must meet the following criteria: authentic subjects, warm colour treatment consistent with the brand palette, and a clear compositional relationship to the brand message. If in doubt, use a brand colour background with typographic treatment — this is always the safer, stronger choice.

<Info>
  For animated ad formats (HTML5 or GIF), the final frame must always be a static brand-colour frame with the logo, tagline, and CTA visible. Animation duration should not exceed 15 seconds, and ads must not loop more than three times.
</Info>
