The Alchemetryx logo is the single most recognisable asset the brand owns. It carries the full weight of the brand's identity — its energy, precision, and sense of transformation — in a single mark. Every time the logo appears, whether on a pitch deck, a website header, or a printed report cover, it should feel intentional and consistent. Treating the logo with care is not a design formality; it is how you protect the trust and recognition the brand builds over time.

## Logo Variants

Four approved logo variants exist to ensure the mark works across every context — light interfaces, dark backgrounds, monochrome production, and brand-forward applications. Always choose the variant that delivers the highest contrast and clearest legibility for the surface it appears on.

## Clear Space

The logo must always breathe. Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the full cap-height of the letter **'A'** in the Alchemetryx wordmark on all four sides — top, bottom, left, and right. This measurement scales proportionally with the logo, so the rule holds at every approved size.

Never crowd the logo with body copy, other logos, decorative elements, or UI components inside this exclusion zone. Clear space is not wasted space — it is what gives the mark authority and prevents it from competing with surrounding content. When in doubt, give the logo more room rather than less.

## Minimum Size

Reproducing the logo below its minimum size compromises legibility, particularly for the wordmark's finer letterforms. Observe these minimum dimensions in all applications:

| Context | Minimum Width (Digital) | Minimum Width (Print) |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | 120px | 30mm |
| Small | 80px | 20mm |
| Minimum | 60px | 15mm |

At or below the minimum size, consider using the logomark (the icon alone) rather than the full wordmark lockup, provided brand context makes the mark identifiable without the accompanying text.

## File Formats

Always use the correct file format for the medium. Using a low-resolution raster file in a print context, or an oversized bitmap in a web context, undermines both the logo's quality and site performance.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Digital">
    **SVG (Scalable Vector Graphic)** is the required format for all web and digital UI applications. SVGs are resolution-independent, meaning the logo will render crisp and sharp at any screen density — including high-DPI and Retina displays — without any increase in file size.

    Use **PNG at 2× resolution** for contexts where SVG is not supported or appropriate, such as social media profile images, email signatures, and presentation decks. Export at double the intended display size (for example, 240px wide if displaying at 120px) to ensure sharpness on high-density screens.

    Avoid JPEG for logo files under any circumstances — the lossy compression introduces artefacts around edges and degrades colour fidelity.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Print">
    **EPS (Encapsulated PostScript)** or **PDF vector** files are the required formats for all print production. These formats preserve the logo as mathematical paths, ensuring the printer can reproduce it at any scale — from a business card to a billboard — without any loss of quality.

    Supply print files to vendors with embedded fonts and outlined paths to prevent font-substitution issues. Always confirm the colour profile with your print vendor: use CMYK builds for standard offset and digital print, and specify Pantone equivalents where spot-colour printing is available.

    Never supply raster files (PNG, JPEG) to a print vendor unless the production method explicitly requires it (for example, dye-sublimation fabric printing).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Dos & Don'ts

Consistent, correct logo usage builds brand equity over time. The guidelines below are not suggestions — they are the standard every team member, agency partner, and vendor should follow without exception.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="✅ Do" icon="check" href="/brand/logo#dos">
    - Use only the four approved logo variants as provided in the brand asset library.
    - Maintain the minimum clear space on all sides at every application size.
    - Scale the logo proportionally, preserving the original aspect ratio at all times.
    - Choose the variant that delivers the highest contrast for the specific background colour or texture in use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="❌ Don't" icon="xmark" href="/brand/logo#donts">
    - Distort, stretch, squish, or rotate the logo in any direction.
    - Recolour the logo using any colour outside the approved Alchemetryx palette.
    - Place the logo on busy, photographic, or patterned backgrounds that reduce legibility.
    - Add drop shadows, glows, gradients, outlines, or any other visual effects to the mark.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
