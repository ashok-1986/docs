Alchemetryx is a Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory that helps owner-led businesses achieve operational clarity. We audit the technology a business already owns, decide what is actually earning its place, and build the one system worth building. If the right answer is to do nothing, we say so. This page is the canonical summary of the brand, aligned to the Brand Document 2026 (v2.0, ratified 13 September 2026).

## What We Do

Alchemetryx does not sell more technology. We measure what a business has already spent on AI and automation, state plainly what to keep and what to remove, and rebuild the process that is costing the business most so it runs without the owner. Our commercial architecture is fixed and public in structure: Diagnostic, then Build, then Retainer. We do not offer outcome-based fees until we have twenty delivered engagements and defensible benchmarks.

We are a consultancy, not an agency, software vendor, or IT support provider. We sit as the independent layer between SME technology spend and business outcomes.

## Core Tension We Solve

SMEs are accumulating digital tools faster than they are building the capability to integrate, govern, measure, and simplify them. The result is hidden costs, duplicated effort, and processes that only one person understands. We fix one process at a time, prove it works, and then move to the next.

## Primary Headline

**One process. Running without you. In 90 days.**

This is the approved primary headline for all external surfaces. It states an outcome, is time-bound, and works for both audience levels defined below.

**Primary ICP:** 25–150 employees (UK). Secondary outreach: 10–24 employees where operational pain is clear. Any engagement outside this range requires executive approval.

**Primary sectors:** Professional Services; Construction and Property; Manufacturing. Secondary: Healthcare and social care, B2B retail and e-commerce, SME-facing Financial Services, Logistics.

## Values

| Value | Operational Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Clarity over cleverness** | If language does not simplify a decision, remove it. |
| **Systems before scale** | Do not accelerate chaos; structure precedes growth. |
| **Outcomes over output** | Measure changed behaviour and decisions, not deliverable volume. |
| **Restraint over hype** | Fewer tools, words, and promises. Name the cost of delay without manufactured urgency. |
| **Long-term trust over short-term wins** | Decline poor-fit work and protect durable partnerships. |
| **Truthful proof** | Never present an invented statistic, engagement count, or result. |
| **Responsible technology** | Respect client data ownership, consent, transparency, fairness, accountability, security, and sustainability. |

## Brand Personality

- **Tone:** Calm, direct, specific, plain-English, practical, observant, quietly confident.
- **Voice:** Treat the reader as intelligent. Lead with a familiar operational problem, then invite a low-friction reply rather than forcing a meeting.
- **Perspective:** Founder-to-founder. We speak as peers who have faced the same operational frustrations.
- **Ethos:** Independent, sceptical, evidence-led, outcome-focused.

## Proof Standard

Every external number must trace to a delivered engagement or a named, linked public source. Unverified claims, fictional client counts, invented research, and unmeasured outcome claims are prohibited.

**Approved proof sources:** delivered client engagements (Care Rota, Diversity Festival), named public sources with links, and Alchemetryx own-builds (Fitosys, clearly labelled as own-build rather than a client engagement).

## Language Rules

**Required:** name the process (rota, invoicing, onboarding, quoting, claims, reporting); name the time it takes; name what it costs where evidenced; short sentences and short paragraphs; one idea at a time; prefer an evidence-backed specific over an adjective.

**Banned:** "operations" as a standalone noun; "intelligence", "operating system", "transformation", "unlock", "empower", "seamless", "ecosystem", "journey"; generic "solutions"; any adjective doing work a number should do; fear-of-missing-out tactics; manufactured urgency.
