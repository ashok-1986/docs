Print materials are often the first physical touchpoint a prospect has with Alchemetryx. A business card handed across a table, a proposal left behind after a client meeting, a brochure picked up at a conference — each of these objects carries the full weight of your brand in a tangible, lasting way. They should feel premium, purposeful, and instantly recognisable as Alchemetryx: considered in their design, precise in their execution, and aligned to the same visual system that governs everything you publish digitally. Never treat print as an afterthought.

## Business Cards

The Alchemetryx business card is a quiet signal of quality. It should feel substantial in the hand and immediately communicate the brand's confidence and precision.

**Specifications**

- **Size:** 85×55mm (standard international format)
- **Stock:** 400gsm silk laminate minimum; soft-touch matte laminate preferred
- **Finish:** Consider a spot UV or foil accent on the Alchemetryx logo for premium print runs

**Front Face**

The front of the card uses an Off-White (`#f9f5f0`) background with a solid Orange (`#ef7025`) accent bar running the full width across the top edge (8mm height). Layout from top to bottom:

- Name: Inter Semi-Bold, 11pt, Deep Navy
- Title: Inter Regular, 9pt, Teal (`#4fa1ab`)
- Email address: Inter Regular, 8pt, Dark Charcoal
- Phone number: Inter Regular, 8pt, Dark Charcoal

Maintain at least 4mm of clear space from all edges. The Alchemetryx wordmark sits in the bottom-right corner at a minimum size of 20mm wide.

**Back Face**

The back of the card uses either Deep Navy (`#1a1f36`) or Teal (`#4fa1ab`) as a full-bleed background. The Alchemetryx logo — wordmark or combined mark — is centred on the card in white. No additional text appears on the back. The back is a pure brand moment.

**Typography Minimums**

- Minimum body font size: 8pt
- Minimum logo size: 20mm wide
- No font other than Inter is permitted on business cards

<Note>
  Always provide both a horizontal and vertical layout variant of the business card for flexibility across regions. Some international contacts prefer vertical-format cards — having both options prepared avoids delays.
</Note>

## Proposals & Reports

A proposal is not just a document — it is an experience. The way Alchemetryx presents its thinking on paper should reflect the same rigour and clarity as the thinking itself. Follow these five steps every time you produce a branded proposal or formal report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cover Page">
    Design the cover page with a full-bleed Deep Navy (`#1a1f36`) background. Centre the Alchemetryx logo in white in the upper third of the page. Below the logo, place the document title in Inter Semi-Bold 28pt white text, followed by the client name in Inter Regular 18pt white, and the date in Inter Regular 12pt Peach (`#eec9bc`). The cover should feel authoritative and premium — resist the temptation to add decorative elements. Negative space is an asset here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inside Cover">
    The inside cover (page 2) is dedicated to context and trust-building. Include the Alchemetryx mission statement in Playfair Display Italic 16pt, centred, in Deep Navy text on an Off-White background. Beneath it, include a one-paragraph brand promise statement that speaks directly to the client's transformation journey. This is not a table of contents — it is a statement of intent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Body Pages">
    All body pages use an Off-White (`#f9f5f0`) background. A solid Orange (`#ef7025`) accent bar runs vertically along the left margin — 8px wide, full page height, anchored to the margin. This is the visual thread that runs through the entire document and marks every page as unmistakably Alchemetryx.

    Typography for body pages:
    - Body text: Inter Regular 11pt, Dark Charcoal (`#2d2d2d`), 1.5 line spacing
    - H1 section titles: Inter Semi-Bold 20pt, Deep Navy
    - H2 subsection titles: Inter Semi-Bold 14pt, Teal (`#4fa1ab`)
    - Pull quotes: Playfair Display Italic 14pt, Orange (`#ef7025`), indented 12mm from left margin
    - Bullet points: use dashes (—) rather than round bullets for a more refined typographic feel
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Pages">
    Pages containing charts, graphs, tables, or quantitative analysis follow all body page rules but apply the following additional standards. All chart series use Teal (`#4fa1ab`) as the primary colour, with Peach (`#eec9bc`) and a mid-weight tint of Teal for secondary and tertiary series. Every chart must include a clear title (Inter Semi-Bold 12pt), labelled axes, and a one-to-two sentence interpretive caption directly beneath the chart in Inter Italic 9pt. Tables use a Teal header row with white text, and alternating Off-White and white row backgrounds for legibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back Cover">
    Mirror the authority of the cover page on the back. Use a Deep Navy full-bleed background. Centred in the lower half of the page: the Alchemetryx tagline in Playfair Display Italic 18pt white, followed by the primary contact name, email, and phone number in Inter Regular 11pt Peach. The Alchemetryx logo appears at the top of the back cover in white, smaller than on the front — approximately 35mm wide. The back cover should feel like a confident, quiet close.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Brochures & One-Pagers

Brochures and one-pagers are concise persuasion tools. They are often the last thing a prospect reads before making a decision to engage — or not. Design them with economy and intention.

**Layout Principles**

- **Column structure:** Two columns maximum on A4 or letter format. For square or landscape formats, a single wide column with strong visual hierarchy performs better than attempting three columns.
- **Headline treatment:** Lead with a bold Orange (`#ef7025`) headline in Inter Semi-Bold 24–28pt. The headline is the hook — it must be specific, benefit-led, and aligned to a transformation outcome.
- **Iconography:** Use only icons from the approved Alchemetryx icon set (Font Awesome Solid weight, Teal colour). Do not mix icon styles or introduce icons from third-party libraries without approval.
- **Body copy:** Inter Regular 10–11pt, Dark Charcoal, 1.4 line spacing. Keep paragraphs short — three to four sentences maximum. Respect the reader's time.
- **CTA placement:** Every brochure or one-pager must end with a clear, unambiguous call to action. Use an Orange CTA button treatment (even in print — a bordered orange box with orange text reads as a CTA), followed by the relevant URL, email, or phone number.
- **Bleed and margins:** Always design with 3mm bleed on all sides. Maintain a minimum 10mm safety margin for all live text and logos.

## Event Materials

Alchemetryx appears at industry events, client engagements, and conferences. Every piece of physical material in those environments contributes to the brand impression — from the banner behind the stand to the name badge around someone's neck.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Banners & Pull-Up Displays" icon="rectangle-wide" href="/brand/print-guidelines#banners">
    Pull-up banners must use a Deep Navy or Teal full-bleed background with the Alchemetryx logo prominent in the upper third. Include the tagline in Playfair Display Italic white text in the centre third, and a URL or contact detail in the lower fifth. Standard pull-up size: 850×2000mm. Ensure a minimum 50mm clear space around the logo at all times.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Name Badges" icon="id-card" href="/brand/print-guidelines#name-badges">
    Name badges for Alchemetryx-hosted events use an Off-White background with an Orange accent bar at the top (12mm height). Attendee name in Inter Semi-Bold 14pt Deep Navy, organisation in Inter Regular 10pt Teal. The Alchemetryx logo sits in the bottom-right corner at 20mm wide. Use 86×54mm landscape format for standard conference badge holders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notebooks & Merchandise" icon="book" href="/brand/print-guidelines#merchandise">
    Branded notebooks and merchandise should prioritise quality over volume. A soft-touch A5 notebook with the Alchemetryx wordmark debossed or foil-stamped on the cover communicates the brand better than a high-volume budget item. Merchandise colour palette: Deep Navy, Teal, or Off-White as base colours with Orange as an accent. Never produce merchandise in non-brand colours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Signage & Wayfinding" icon="sign-hanging" href="/brand/print-guidelines#signage">
    Directional and environmental signage at Alchemetryx events must maintain brand colour discipline. Use Deep Navy backgrounds for primary directional signs and Teal for secondary information signs. All signage typography uses Inter Semi-Bold in white. Logo placement on signage follows the standard clear space rules. For large-format signage above 1500mm wide, increase logo size proportionally — logo must never appear smaller than 80mm wide on any sign.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Print Production Notes

<Warning>
  Always provide print files in CMYK colour mode. Never send RGB files to a print production vendor — colours will shift unpredictably, and the resulting materials will not accurately represent the Alchemetryx brand. Request a physical proof before committing to any print run larger than 50 units. Approve the proof in person where possible; PDF proofs do not accurately represent printed colour.
</Warning>

**CMYK Colour Values**

When supplying files to print vendors, use the following CMYK values to ensure accurate colour reproduction across all print materials:

| Colour Name | Hex | C | M | Y | K |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alchemetryx Orange | #ef7025 | 0 | 58 | 90 | 0 |
| Alchemetryx Peach | #eec9bc | 0 | 18 | 18 | 0 |
| Alchemetryx Teal | #4fa1ab | 54 | 5 | 0 | 33 |
| Deep Navy | #1a1f36 | 44 | 44 | 0 | 82 |

**File Format Requirements**

- All print-ready files must be supplied as PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 format
- Minimum image resolution: 300 DPI at final print size
- All fonts must be embedded or outlined before submission
- Bleed: 3mm on all sides for all formats
- Include crop marks and colour bars in final print files

<Info>
  If you are working with an external designer or print supplier, share this page along with the brand colour values and font specifications. Do not assume external partners are familiar with the Alchemetryx brand standards — always brief them explicitly and review files before sending to production.
</Info>
