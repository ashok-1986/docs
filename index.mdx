## How to Use This Hub

Follow these three steps to move from brand understanding to confident execution — whether you're a new team member getting up to speed or a strategist preparing a client campaign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with Foundation">
    Begin by reading the Mission, Vision & Values page. Before you apply any framework or design system, you need to understand *why* Alchemetryx exists — the transformation it promises, the problems it solves, and the principles that govern every decision. Everything else in this hub flows from that foundation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Absorb the Visual System">
    Move into the Brand Overview to learn how the Alchemetryx identity looks and feels in practice. Study the color palette — from the bold energy of the primary orange to the clarity of the teal and the warmth of the soft peach — alongside typography choices and logo usage rules. Consistent visuals build the trust that strategy alone cannot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Strategy">
    With the brand foundation and visual system in hand, turn to the ICP and Go-To-Market documentation. Use the ICP definition to sharpen your targeting, the positioning frameworks to craft compelling messaging, and the GTM playbooks to design campaigns that convert. This is where brand identity becomes measurable business growth.
  </Step>
</Steps>
