Alchemetryx sells founder-to-founder to owner-led UK SMEs of 25 to 150 staff, with a parallel India track. This page describes the three real personas we talk to and the messaging hooks that work for each. It replaces any earlier persona sets, including any framing built around invented job titles or fictional quotes.

## Persona 1: The Owner-Led Operator (primary)

The primary buyer. Owner, CEO, or founding partner of a UK firm with 25 to 150 employees and £500K to £5M in revenue. Runs the business day-to-day and still touches the same slow process every week.

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | Owner, CEO, or founding partner |
| **Firm size** | 25 to 150 employees, £500K to £5M revenue |
| **Reports to** | No one internal. Answers to family, board, or the bank |
| **Top frustrations** | Manual work, disconnected tools, weak visibility, decisions still routing through them |
| **Buying triggers** | A key person on leave, a lost deal, a compliance scare, a failed automation attempt |
| **Objections** | Scepticism of hype, tool fatigue, distrust of consultants who over-promise |
| **What makes them buy** | Evidence, a fixed price, a named process, a safe first step |

**Messaging hooks:**

- Level 1: "Start with one process. AI is what makes it run without you."
- Level 2: "You bought the AI. Did anything change?"
- Universal: "One process. Running without you. In 90 days."

**What NOT to say:** never use transformation, unlock, empower, seamless, ecosystem, journey, or generic solutions. Never manufacture urgency. Never quote a rate card.

## Persona 2: The COO or Operations Lead (champion)

Common in the upper end of the ICP (100 to 150 staff). Not the final decision-maker, but the person who sells the engagement internally and owns the process being rebuilt.

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | COO, Operations Director, Head of Operations |
| **Firm size** | 100 to 150 employees, £2M to £5M revenue |
| **Motivations** | Get the owner off critical-path work, stop the team firefighting the same recurring issues |
| **Buying triggers** | A repeated month-end failure, high staff turnover on a manual process, a failed internal automation attempt |
| **Objections** | Tool fatigue, prior consulting engagements that produced decks and nothing else |
| **What makes them buy** | A one-page Decision Brief they can walk into the owner's office with, a fixed price, a named go-live date |

**Messaging hooks:** lead with the Decision Brief and the fixed-price rebuild. The COO needs internal ammunition. Give them a written artefact, not a sales pitch.

## Persona 3: The Founder or Promoter (India, parallel track)

India is a distinct parallel track with different firmographics, compliance triggers, and pricing. The persona summary below is for the India buyer. The full India play is developed separately.

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | Founder, Promoter, or CFO |
| **Firm size** | 50 to 250 employees, ₹5Cr to ₹50Cr revenue |
| **Project budget** | ₹5L to ₹50L |
| **Sector framing** | Do **not** mirror UK sector cards. India sector framing is separate. |

<Note>
  Do not port UK case studies, sector language, or headline framing directly into India collateral without explicit adaptation. India has its own proof, its own compliance triggers, and its own routing.
</Note>

## Not a persona

We deliberately do not sell to:

- **Open-ended strategy buyers** who want advisory without a defined process to fix.
- **Firms under 10 employees** where the owner is the only operator and budget is thin.
- **Prospects who refuse case-study participation.** Current operating rules require the buyer to be open to a published or detailed anonymised case study. If they refuse, we walk.
- **Buyers wanting brand-new custom software** built from scratch. We rebuild processes using tools the client already pays for. If a bespoke build is required, we refer to a development partner.
- **Firms over 150 employees** without executive-approved exception.
