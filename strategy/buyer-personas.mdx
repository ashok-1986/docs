Buyer personas are fictional but data-informed representations of the real humans who buy and champion Alchemetryx engagements. They are not demographic abstractions — they are portraits of the motivations, frustrations, and decision-making patterns that show up repeatedly across our best client relationships. Use them to sharpen how you write, pitch, present, and deliver. When you know who you are talking to, every word and every slide gets sharper.

---

## Persona 1: The Strategic Leader

**The decision-maker who sets the agenda and signs the engagement.**

| Quick Stats | |
|---|---|
| Age Range | 38–55 |
| Seniority | C-Suite or Founder |
| Typical Titles | CEO, Founder, Managing Director, President |
| Organisation Size | 50–1,000 employees |
| Typical Industries | Technology Scale-ups, Professional Services, Consumer Brands |

### Goals

- Drive meaningful, sustainable growth — not just incremental revenue, but a step-change in market position, brand strength, or operational capability
- Make better strategic decisions faster by having a clear picture of where the organisation is, where it is going, and what it needs to do next
- Build an organisation that can scale without breaking — where strategy, brand, and data work together rather than pulling in different directions

### Frustrations

- Consultants who deliver a deck, take the fee, and disappear — leaving the organisation with a strategy document nobody implements
- The gap between the quality of the organisation's actual work and the way it shows up in the market: their brand undersells them, their data gives them no clarity, and their growth feels random
- Time: as the most senior person in the room, they are perpetually over-scheduled and under-resourced for strategic thinking — they need a partner who makes complex things simple and moves fast

### What They Need from Alchemetryx

- A clear, prioritised strategy with defined outcomes — not a framework to fill in themselves, but a directional plan they can act on immediately
- A partner who earns the right to challenge their assumptions — someone confident enough to say what they see and credible enough to be believed
- Accountability and momentum: regular check-ins, honest progress reporting, and a team that drives the work forward without needing to be chased

### Messaging Hook

> *"We don't just advise — we deliver transformation you can measure."*

## Persona 2: The Growth Champion

**The internal driver who identifies the need and builds the case for Alchemetryx.**

| Quick Stats | |
|---|---|
| Age Range | 32–48 |
| Seniority | Director or VP |
| Typical Titles | CMO, VP Marketing, Head of Growth, Chief Commercial Officer |
| Organisation Size | 100–2,000 employees |
| Typical Industries | Technology Scale-ups, Financial Services, Consumer Brands |

### Goals

- Prove the commercial value of brand, data, and strategy investment to a leadership team that is more comfortable with revenue metrics than brand equity
- Build a marketing and growth function that is genuinely strategic — one that influences product, pricing, and positioning rather than just executing campaigns
- Find an external partner who brings the rigour and framework they know is necessary but cannot get from a traditional creative agency

### Frustrations

- Agencies that are long on beautiful output and short on strategic thinking — they can produce a brand book but cannot tell you whether it will actually drive growth
- Being positioned as a cost centre rather than a growth driver: they know the work they are championing will move the needle, but they lack the data and the language to prove it in advance
- Internal politics: getting strategic initiatives funded and prioritised requires navigating multiple stakeholders, and they rarely have the bandwidth to fight every battle themselves

### What They Need from Alchemetryx

- Strategic rigour that backs up creative output with evidence, frameworks, and measurable success criteria — the kind of work that survives a CFO's scrutiny
- A partner who helps them build internal credibility: presenting to the board, writing the business case, or simply being in the room to add weight to the recommendation
- Practical tools and deliverables they can use to align the organisation — not just a strategy document, but a messaging guide, a measurement framework, a campaign brief

### Messaging Hook

> *"Get the strategic rigour of a consultancy with the creative output of a world-class agency."*

## Persona 3: The Transformation Owner

**The operational leader who makes the engagement work day-to-day.**

| Quick Stats | |
|---|---|
| Age Range | 30–45 |
| Seniority | Senior Manager to Director |
| Typical Titles | COO, VP Operations, Chief of Staff, Programme Director, Head of Strategy |
| Organisation Size | 200–5,000 employees |
| Typical Industries | Professional Services, Financial Services, Healthcare |

### Goals

- Deliver the engagement on time, within scope, and with clear outcomes — their reputation is on the line as the internal owner of the relationship
- Keep the senior sponsor informed and confident without creating unnecessary noise or escalations — they are the bridge between Alchemetryx and the leadership team
- Ensure the strategy actually lands inside the organisation: that it gets embedded in decisions, adopted by teams, and followed up on after the final presentation

### Frustrations

- Scope creep and moving goalposts — when deliverables are vague, timelines slip, and the engagement loses momentum, they are the ones managing the fallout internally
- Consultants who over-promise in the pitch and under-deliver in the work: the gap between the senior partner who sold the engagement and the junior team that delivers it
- Lack of transparency: they need to know what is happening, what is coming next, and where the risks are — not because they distrust the team, but because they are accountable to people who will ask

### What They Need from Alchemetryx

- A detailed, clearly scoped statement of work with defined milestones, deliverable descriptions, and decision points — something they can share with leadership and use to track progress
- A consistent, senior point of contact who is genuinely across the engagement and can give them a straight answer about status, risks, and next steps at any time
- Practical change management support: help with internal communication, stakeholder alignment, and embedding the strategy so it survives the post-engagement handover

### Messaging Hook

> *"Every Alchemetryx engagement comes with clear deliverables, transparent timelines, and weekly accountability."*

<Note>
  These personas are starting points, not finished portraits. Update them with real data from won deals, lost deal interviews, and client feedback at least twice per year. </Note>
