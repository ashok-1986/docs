Alchemetryx reaches owner-led UK firms through consultative, outbound-first sales backed by a small set of earned channels. This page sets the channel priority order for the current operating phase, the exact outreach language approved for the One Process Rebuild, and the ongoing channel table from the Brand Document. Read this before writing any outreach copy or planning LinkedIn activity.

## Channel priorities (Phase 1)

The next 30–45 days focus on channels with the shortest path to a qualified conversation. Work them in this order:

1. **Direct outreach to care homes and similar rota or staffing-heavy operators.** We have live proof: the Care Rota rebuild. Name the process, name the time, offer to show the example.
2. **Warm introductions and existing relationships.** The highest-converting source for a services business. Prioritise reactivating dormant contacts and asking for one named introduction.
3. **LinkedIn founder-led posts and comments in relevant niches.** Owner: Ashok. Weekly cadence, founder-to-founder voice, evidence-led. See the playbook below.
4. **Follow-up from `/week` quiz completions.** Treat every submission as a warm lead. Map the highest-scoring leak to the One Process Rebuild and offer a short call.

Do not open new channels until these four are operating consistently. Depth in a few channels compounds faster than scattered effort across many.

## Core outreach language

Use this copy in cold emails, LinkedIn messages, and warm introductions. Do not rewrite it into hype or add performance claims. The exact wording is approved from the Operating Plan.

> Most owner-led businesses still have one process that only works because one person remembers how it goes. We rebuild that single process so it runs without them, usually in 3–4 weeks. Fixed price, working system handed over. Happy to show you the care-home rota we rebuilt if useful.
>
> Is there one process right now that is still living in a spreadsheet or someone's head that you'd like to hand over first?

That is the full pitch. Lead with it. If the prospect responds, move to a 20-minute fit call.

## /week quiz follow-up

Every quiz completion is scored against three leaks: Rework, Slow answers, and Manual handoffs. Whichever scores highest is the specific pain to name in follow-up. Do not send a generic "thanks for participating" email. Instead, send one short message that maps the highest leak directly to the One Process Rebuild, names the time frame, and offers a 20-minute call to confirm fit. For example, if Manual handoffs scores highest: "Your quiz results show manual handoffs as the biggest leak. That is exactly what the One Process Rebuild is built to fix: one process mapped, rebuilt, and handed back running without you, in 3–4 weeks. Worth a 20-minute call to see if it fits?"

## LinkedIn founder playbook

Ashok operates the founder profile as a personal channel, not a broadcast platform. Follow these rules:

- **Post once per week.** Consistency beats volume. One thoughtful post outperforms three rushed ones.
- **Founder-to-founder voice.** Write as one owner speaking to another. No corporate third person. No jargon, no hype.
- **Draw from the content pillars.** Use Notes From the Work for field observations and Evidence Desk for proof-backed specifics. Do not invent case studies or outcomes. Approved proof only: Care Rota (cleared), Diversity Festival (documented, pending client approval), Fitosys (own-build, labelled clearly).
- **Avoid banned language.** Never use: transformation, unlock, empower, seamless, ecosystem, journey, or generic "solutions." Never use FOMO or manufactured urgency.
- **Engage in comments daily.** Respond to every comment on your own posts. Spend 15 minutes commenting meaningfully on posts by prospects and peers. Add a data point, ask a follow-up question, or share a relevant experience.
- **No pitch in the post.** The post builds trust. The call to action is a conversation starter or an offer to show the Care Rota example. The sale happens in the DM or on the call, not in the feed.

## Referral partners

The Brand Document names three partner types that align with the ICP without overlapping services:

- **Accounting firms** that serve owner-led businesses at £500K–£5M revenue. They see the financial pain of broken processes first.
- **Banks and business lenders** that work with the same segment. They care about operational resilience because it affects credit risk.
- **Business groups and trade bodies** in primary sectors: Professional Services, Construction and Property, and Manufacturing.

Approach each partner with a clear value exchange: you introduce clients whose processes are costing them money. We rebuild one process and report back on what we found. No referral fee is required in Phase 1. A good working relationship and shared proof points are more valuable than a commission structure at this stage.
