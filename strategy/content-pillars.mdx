Alchemetryx runs four content pillars that together carry the brand voice, build trust with owner-led buyers, and generate the proof points our consultative sales motion depends on. This page defines the four pillars, the governance rules that apply to every piece, the SEO priorities, and the editorial QA checklist. It replaces any earlier three-pillar content plan.

## Content governance rules

Every piece, regardless of pillar, must follow these rules:

- **Human, outcome-led voice.** Founder-to-founder tone. Treat the reader as intelligent.
- **Advice before action.** If the honest advice is to do nothing, say so.
- **No agency jargon.** Never use: operations (as standalone noun), intelligence, operating system, transformation, unlock, empower, seamless, ecosystem, journey, generic solutions.
- **Evidence-backed claims.** Every number must trace to a delivered engagement or a named linked public source.
- **Transparent ethos.** "If the right move is to do nothing, that is exactly what we will recommend."
- **Named process.** Name the process (rota, invoicing, onboarding, quoting, claims, reporting), not the abstract theme.

## Formats per pillar

| Pillar | Opening hook rule | Length | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Evidence Desk | Open with a specific number or a named source. No generic industry hook. | 700 to 1,200 words. | Single CTA to the Diagnostic or the relevant case study. |
| Notes From the Work | Open with a concrete moment from delivery. No abstract lesson upfront. | 150 to 400 words (LinkedIn), 500 to 900 words (blog). | Single CTA: reply to the post, or book a 20-minute call. |
| The Room | Open with the discussion question and who is in the room. | Live session summary of 400 to 800 words. | Single CTA: register for the next session. |
| The Toolkit | Open with the exact problem the template solves. | The template plus 200 to 400 words of usage notes. | Single CTA: download the template (email capture). |

Every piece has one CTA. Never two.

## SEO priorities

**Primary UK keywords:**

- technology audit for SMEs
- AI spend audit
- software rationalisation consultancy
- SME operational efficiency
- process improvement for growing businesses

**Secondary UK keywords:**

- Microsoft 365 audit
- Copilot ROI
- automation effectiveness
- SME technology effectiveness

**Tactics:**

- Publish Evidence Desk articles targeting the primary keywords.
- Optimise case studies for "AI audit [sector]" and "process improvement [sector]".
- Earn backlinks through guest posts in SME publications and trade bodies.

## Editorial QA checklist

Before publishing any external piece, answer **yes** to all of the following. This is adapted from the Brand QA Gate.

1. Is the content aligned to the two-level AI Adoption / AI Usage model or the Diagnostic → Build → Retainer architecture?
2. Does it name a real process and an evidence-backed outcome rather than selling "transformation" or a tool?
3. Is every number attributable, current, and authorised?
4. If visual, are Midnight Sapphire, Alchemical Gold, and Pearl White used correctly, with Urbanist typography?
5. Is the tone direct, calm, specific, and free from hype?
6. Does the piece avoid legacy pilot language, retired prices, fictitious proof, and unverified regulation claims?
7. Does it have one clear CTA and no conflicting next steps?
8. Does it include UK company number 17199377 where appropriate?
