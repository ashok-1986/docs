The Alchemetryx go-to-market strategy is built on a simple premise — earn trust before asking for business. We lead with insight, demonstrate expertise, and let our work do the selling. In a market crowded with consultancies and agencies all claiming to be strategic, the most powerful differentiator is the consistent, generous demonstration of exactly the kind of thinking clients will get when they hire us. The GTM motion is not a funnel to be forced — it is a relationship to be built, at pace, with discipline.

## GTM Principles

These three principles underpin every channel decision, campaign, and outreach we run. When a GTM tactic conflicts with any of them, stop and recalibrate.

  <Card title="Insight-Led" icon="brain" href="#gtm-motion-overview">
    Every GTM touchpoint leads with a relevant insight or perspective — not a sales message. Before we ask a prospect for anything, we give them something useful: a data point, a framework, a perspective they haven't considered. Insight earns attention; attention earns conversation.

  <Card title="Relationship-Centred" icon="people-arrows" href="#gtm-motion-overview">

## GTM Motion Overview

The Alchemetryx GTM motion moves prospects through five stages — from first awareness to long-term partnership. Each stage has a clear purpose, a defined set of activities, and a transition criterion before moving to the next.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attract — Create Awareness Among Ideal Clients">
    Build consistent visibility among mid-market B2B leaders through thought leadership content, LinkedIn presence, SEO-optimised articles, and speaking appearances. The goal at this stage is not to sell — it is to become a familiar, trusted voice in the spaces where our ideal clients learn and make sense of their challenges. Every piece of content should leave a reader thinking: *these people understand my world.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage — Start High-Value Conversations">
    Convert awareness into conversation through discovery calls, diagnostic workshops, and free insight sessions. The diagnostic workshop is the Alchemetryx signature engagement tool — it gives prospects a structured, facilitated experience of our thinking before any commercial commitment. Engagement activities should be low-friction, high-value, and clearly framed around the prospect's goals rather than a sales agenda.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert — Turn Conversations Into Engagements">
    Present tailored proposals that are grounded in the specific context surfaced during the engagement stage. Use case studies and client outcomes to build confidence, and frame the investment in terms of the ROI the engagement is designed to generate. Every proposal should feel like it was written for this specific client — because it should be. Generic proposals signal a generic approach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver — Execute With Rigour and Visibility">
    Structured project delivery with weekly client communications, milestone reviews, and a measurable outcomes framework established from day one. Delivery is not a separate phase from GTM — it is the most powerful brand communication Alchemetryx produces. Clients who experience excellent delivery refer and expand. Every delivered engagement is a future case study and a future referral source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand & Refer — Build Long-Term Value">
    On project completion, transition clients into the referral programme and the retainer conversation. Create a case study with the client's involvement — this cements the relationship and produces a sales asset simultaneously. Identify the next strategic opportunity within the client's organisation and introduce a retainer model that keeps Alchemetryx embedded as the ongoing strategic partner. A retained client is the highest-value GTM outcome Alchemetryx can achieve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revenue Model

Alchemetryx operates across four engagement types. Understanding the revenue model helps the GTM team qualify opportunities accurately and match the right engagement structure to each client need.

| Engagement Type | Description | Typical Value | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Project** | A defined strategic transformation project with a clear scope, deliverables, and outcomes framework. Typical examples include brand strategy, growth strategy, data strategy, or integrated transformation programmes. | £25,000 – £150,000 | 6 weeks – 6 months |
| **Retainer** | An ongoing strategic partnership providing consistent advisory, execution, and strategic review. The highest-value engagement type for both client and Alchemetryx — produces the deepest transformation and the strongest relationship. | £5,000 – £20,000 / month | 6–24 months |
| **Workshop** | A standalone diagnostic or strategy day that delivers focused insight and prioritised recommendations in a single intensive session. Often the entry point into a larger engagement. | £3,500 – £10,000 | 1–2 days |
| **Advisory** | A fractional strategic advisor relationship providing senior strategic input on a time-allocated basis. Suited to clients who have internal execution resource but need external strategic leadership. | £2,500 – £8,000 / month | 3–12 months |
