Alchemetryx is a Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory. Our go-to-market model is built around consultative, founder-to-founder conversations. The market for what we do is not yet searching. We must educate before we sell. That means a high-touch, low-volume approach with proof and trust at its centre.

This page describes the ratified growth model and the current 30–45 day operating focus. It replaces any earlier pipeline projections or retired positioning.

## Growth model

We operate two tracks in parallel.

**Primary: Consultative Sales (Outbound-First)**

This is our core engine. We identify owner-led firms that match our ICP and start a conversation directly. The mindset is founder-to-founder. We diagnose a specific process, name the time and cost, and propose a clear next step. We do not wait for inbound demand because the firms we serve are not yet searching for a "technology effectiveness audit." They are running a business and wondering why things feel harder than they should.

**Secondary: Content-Led Demand Generation**

Content supports trust and timing. Our four content pillars generate proof, demonstrate observational depth, and keep us visible to prospects who may be months away from a decision. Content does not replace outreach. It makes the outreach warmer when the timing is right.

## Model characteristics

| Characteristic | How we apply it |
| --- | --- |
| High-touch, low-volume | We target a small number of the right firms and invest time in understanding their operations |
| Long sales cycles | Education comes before a proposal. A first meeting may lead to an engagement three to six months later |
| Trust-based | Every claim traces to a delivered engagement or a named public source. No invented outcomes |
| Value-based pricing | We price against the value of a rebuilt process, not day rates or time-and-materials |
| Process-named | We name the exact process (rota, invoicing, onboarding, quoting, claims, reporting) in every conversation |

## Current phase (next 30–45 days)

We are executing a time-boxed operating plan. The goal is survival plus proof. We are running a lighter MVP offer that sits **under** the full Brand Document architecture, not in place of it.

### Phase 1: Cash and proof

The operating focus is on securing paid engagements quickly, building documented case evidence, and proving the model before scaling.

**MVP Offer: One Process Rebuild**

- **Scope:** One process, rebuilt and running
- **Price:** £5,950 excluding VAT (fixed)
- **Absolute floor:** £4,950 with founder approval only
- **Duration:** 3 to 4 weeks
- **Payment:** 50% on kick-off, 50% on go-live
- **Support:** 30 days settling support included
- **Target:** 2 to 3 paid rebuilds closed by Day 45
- **Retainer Bridge:** £750 to £1,200 per month light retainer after the settling period
- **Conversion target:** At least 60% of rebuild clients into the light retainer

This is a lighter entry retainer than the full Brand Document retainer (£2,000 to £3,500 per month). The retainer bridge is designed to maintain momentum and build the case for the full Diagnostic, Build, and Retainer architecture once proof is established.

**Fallback offer: Diagnostic + Decision Brief**

- **Price:** £2,950
- **Duration:** Approximately 10 working days
- **Output:** The Decision Brief. One page: What is working? What is not? Options considered? What we advise and why? What happens if you do nothing?

### Phase 2: Full architecture activation

We activate the full Diagnostic (£3,500) to Build (£8,000 to £15,000) to Retainer (£2,000 to £3,500 per month) architecture at higher intensity **only after** Phase 1 has delivered proof in the form of signed case studies, documented outcomes, and retainer conversions.

<Note>
  The MVP offer is a temporary operating measure. The full offer architecture defined in the Brand Document remains the long-term model.
</Note>

## Hard operating rules for Phase 1

- **Price floor:** £5,950 for the One Process Rebuild. £4,950 is the exceptional floor and requires founder approval.
- **Capacity ceiling:** No more than 2 concurrent rebuild projects at any time.
- **Case-study filter:** Every engagement must agree to a documented case study. If a prospect refuses, we walk away.
- **No pure custom work:** We sell a defined rebuild, not open-ended consultancy.
- **Friday review cadence:** Every Friday, we review pipeline, capacity, and proof against the Phase 1 targets.
- **50% upfront:** We collect 50% payment before kick-off on every engagement.
- **Proof standard absolute:** Every external number, claim, or outcome must trace to a delivered engagement or a named linked public source. No exceptions.

## Kill and pivot triggers

We will stop the current operating plan and reassess if any of the following occur:

- Zero paid engagements secured by Day 30
- Two or more failed engagements (client drops out or refuses to pay balance)
- No signed case studies by Day 45
- Conversion to light retainer falls below 40%
- Founder capacity is breached by more than 20% for two consecutive weeks
- A major market event makes the ICP definition obsolete

If a kill trigger is hit, we return to the Brand Document for a revised operating plan. We do not scale a broken model.
