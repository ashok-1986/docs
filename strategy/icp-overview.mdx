Alchemetryx serves owner-led UK businesses of 25–150 employees who either have no deliberate AI or automation system in place, or who have bought tools without ever measuring whether they changed a business result. This page is the canonical ICP, ratified in the Brand Document 2026 (v2.0, 13 September 2026). It supersedes every earlier employee range, industry card, and readiness heuristic.

## Two-Level Routing

Every qualified prospect is routed into one of two levels. The routing decides the message, the framing of the Diagnostic, and the specific language used in outreach.

| Level | Definition | Core tension | Message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 1: AI Adoption** | Owner-led UK firms of 25–150 staff operating through people, email, and spreadsheets. No deliberate AI or automation system. | "AI is for larger companies." Data and processes feel messy. | Start with one process. AI is what makes it run without you. |
| **Level 2: AI Usage** | Firms of 25–150 staff already using Copilot, ChatGPT, Zapier, or prior automation, but unable to show a measured return. | Tools were bought. System performance was never measured. | You bought the AI. Did anything change? |

## Sector Focus

**Primary sectors** (largest gap between AI adoption and integration, per UK Business Data Survey):

1. Professional Services (legal, accountancy, consulting, architecture, engineering)
2. Construction and Property (development, management, specialist B2B services)

**Secondary sectors:** Healthcare and social care, B2B retail and e-commerce, SME-facing Financial Services, Logistics.

## Persona Snapshot: The Owner-Led Operator

The primary reader is a busy owner or CEO of a growing business who experiences:

- Manual work that never lets up
- Disconnected tools with no shared source of truth
- Weak visibility across teams and processes
- A founder or leadership bottleneck where too many decisions still route through one person

They are sceptical of technology hype, want evidence rather than capability claims, and value a safe, clear route to a measurable outcome. See [Buyer Personas](/strategy/buyer-personas) for the full decision-maker, champion, and budget-holder profiles.

## Qualification Signals

Alchemetryx is a fit when several of these are true:

- One high-pain process still lives in someone's head or a spreadsheet: rota, intake, quoting, handovers, reporting, onboarding.
- The owner is a bottleneck for a specific recurring decision or approval.
- Tools have been bought (Microsoft 365, Copilot, ChatGPT, Zapier, a CRM) but no one can point to the business result they changed.
- The buyer is open to a published or detailed anonymised case study with measurable before-and-after.
- Budget for £5K–£25K of project work exists without a lengthy internal approval chain.

## Disqualifiers

Alchemetryx is **not** a fit when:

- The prospect wants open-ended strategy or advisory work with no defined process to fix.
- The scope cannot be reduced to one process delivered inside a fixed box.
- The prospect is buying capacity to build new software they do not currently pay for.
- The prospect refuses any form of case-study participation. Under current hard operating rules, we walk.
- The buyer is more than 150 employees without executive-approved exception, or under 10 employees.

## India (Parallel Track)

India is a distinct parallel track with its own compliance triggers, proof, pricing, and routing. **Do not mirror UK sector language into India.** India firmographics:

- Employee count: 50–250
- Revenue: ₹5Cr–₹50Cr
- Project budget: ₹5L–₹50L
- Decision-maker: Founder/Promoter, CFO
