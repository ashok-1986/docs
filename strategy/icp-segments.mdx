Alchemetryx focuses on three primary UK sectors where the gap between AI adoption and integration is widest and where one high-pain process usually justifies the whole engagement. This page breaks down each primary sector with firmographics, typical triggers, and the shape of a first engagement. India is treated as a distinct parallel track with different firmographics and sector framing.

## Primary sectors

### Professional Services

Legal, accountancy, consulting, architecture, and engineering firms with 25 to 150 staff and £500K to £5M in revenue. The billable hour and the client file are the two pressure points. Quoting, matter or job intake, onboarding, and reporting typically live in a mix of practice management software, spreadsheets, and email.

- **Firmographics:** 25 to 150 employees, £500K to £5M revenue, owner or managing partner as decision-maker.
- **Typical triggers:** quoting takes days and margins are inconsistent, onboarding a new client eats a full day of partner time, monthly reporting is copied between spreadsheets.
- **Level 1 framing:** "Start with one process. AI is what makes it run without you." Rebuild quoting or onboarding first.
- **Level 2 framing:** "You bought the AI. Did anything change?" Audit Copilot and Zapier usage before renewing licences.
- **Tools already owned:** Microsoft 365, Copilot, a practice management platform, a CRM, occasional Zapier flows.
- **First engagement:** typically Diagnostic + Decision Brief (£2,950) followed by One Process Rebuild (£5,950) on the process the Decision Brief names.

### Construction and Property

Development, property management, and specialist B2B services with 25 to 150 staff. Site handovers, subcontractor scheduling, quoting, and safety reporting are common pain points. Integration rates in construction sit at around 14% (UK Business Data Survey), which means most firms have tools but little connection between them.

- **Firmographics:** 25 to 150 employees, £500K to £5M revenue, owner, MD, or operations director as decision-maker.
- **Typical triggers:** subcontractor rota lives in one person's head, project handovers rely on WhatsApp, quoting varies by estimator, safety reporting is manual and always late.
- **Level 1 framing:** rebuild scheduling or handovers first.
- **Level 2 framing:** audit existing project software and any bespoke integrations before adding more.
- **Tools already owned:** Microsoft 365, project management platforms (Procore, Fieldwire, or similar), accounting software, spreadsheets for scheduling.
- **First engagement:** One Process Rebuild (£5,950) on the highest-pain process, then Retainer Bridge (£750 to £1,200 per month).

### Manufacturing

SME manufacturers with 25 to 250 employees. Production scheduling, quote-to-order, quality reporting, and stock reconciliation typically bottleneck around the owner or plant manager. Integration rates in manufacturing sit at around 12% (UK Business Data Survey).

- **Firmographics:** 25 to 250 employees (upper end sits above the standard ICP; still primary within this sector), £500K to £5M revenue, owner, MD, or COO as decision-maker.
- **Typical triggers:** stock reconciliation is a weekly manual exercise, quoting requires the owner's approval on every job, quality reports are compiled by hand at month-end.
- **Level 1 framing:** rebuild the quote-to-order flow or scheduling first.
- **Level 2 framing:** audit MRP or ERP add-ons and any automation attempts.
- **Tools already owned:** Microsoft 365, an MRP or ERP system, accounting software, spreadsheets for scheduling and stock.
- **First engagement:** Diagnostic (£3,500) to establish the baseline, then Build (£8,000 to £15,000) for the rebuild.

## Secondary sectors

Secondary sectors are worked opportunistically when the operational pain is clear and matches an existing proof point.

| Sector | Typical entry process |
| --- | --- |
| Healthcare and social care | Rota, staff scheduling, compliance reporting (proof: Care Rota). |
| B2B retail and e-commerce | Order intake, fulfilment handover, returns reconciliation. |
| SME-facing Financial Services | Client onboarding, KYC handover, monthly client reporting. |
| Logistics | Job dispatch, driver scheduling, proof-of-delivery capture. |

## India (parallel track)

India uses different firmographics and different sector framing. Do not mirror UK sector cards into India collateral.

- **Employee count:** 50 to 250
- **Revenue:** ₹5Cr to ₹50Cr
- **Project budget:** ₹5L to ₹50L
- **Decision-makers:** Founder or Promoter, CFO

India pricing on the full offer: The Diagnostic at ₹2,50,000 (full) or ₹1,25,000 (single-function); The Build at ₹8,00,000 to ₹15,00,000; The Retainer at ₹2,00,000 to ₹3,50,000 per month. India sector framing is developed separately and is not covered by this page.
