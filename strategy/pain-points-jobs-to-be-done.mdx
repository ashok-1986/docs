Understanding why customers really hire Alchemetryx — beyond the stated need — is the foundation of every winning pitch and every satisfied client. A prospect rarely calls and says "I need a brand strategy." They say "our pipeline has dried up" or "we keep losing to competitors who seem less capable than us" or "we have all this data and I still can't tell the board what is working." The pain is real and specific. The job they are hiring us to do is deeper than the service we deliver. When you understand both layers, you can sell more clearly, scope more accurately, and deliver more meaningfully.

## Top Pain Points

These are the six most common pain points that bring organisations to Alchemetryx. They appear across sectors, seniority levels, and engagement types — and each one represents a genuine strategic crisis for the organisations experiencing it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Strategy Paralysis" icon="circle-question">
    Leaders have data, market awareness, and genuine ambition — but no clarity on what to prioritise or where to direct growth investment next. They are stuck at the intersection of too many options and too little confidence in which path is right. Every quarter passes with activity but no strategic momentum.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brand Invisibility" icon="eye-slash">
    Their brand does not reflect their actual quality, expertise, or ambition — and it is costing them deals. Prospects underestimate them. Partners hesitate. Talent looks elsewhere. The organisation has outgrown its identity but has not yet built the one it deserves. Every touchpoint is an undersell.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Without Direction" icon="database">
    They have dashboards, analytics platforms, and years of accumulated data — but no one is turning that data into decisions. Reports get filed. Numbers get reviewed. Nothing changes. The gap is not the data; it is the analytical thinking, the frameworks, and the habits that would make data a strategic asset rather than a reporting burden.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The Execution Gap" icon="angles-right">
    Strategy gets created — sometimes brilliantly — but it never lands. Plans sit in decks. Priorities get diluted by day-to-day pressure. The organisation cycles through strategy exercises without ever seeing transformation. The problem is not the thinking; it is the absence of a disciplined, accountable implementation engine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Talent Limitations" icon="users">
    They do not have — and cannot cost-effectively hire — the internal strategic, creative, and analytical talent that transformation requires. Building a team of senior strategists, brand experts, and data analysts is a multi-year investment and a cultural shift. They need the capability now, not in three years.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Growth Plateau" icon="chart-line">
    Revenue has stalled and the leadership team cannot diagnose why. Is it a brand problem? A channel problem? A product-market fit problem? A pricing problem? They are trying everything and nothing is moving the needle — which is both commercially dangerous and deeply demoralising for a team that is used to growing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Jobs-to-be-Done Framework

The Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework, developed by Clayton Christensen and extended by practitioners including Bob Moesta, holds that customers do not buy products or services — they hire them to do a job. That job has three dimensions: a functional need (what they need to get done), an emotional need (how they want to feel), and a social need (how they want to be seen). Understanding all three dimensions is what separates a pitch that resonates from one that merely informs.

When Alchemetryx maps its engagements to JTBD, the services become secondary to the outcomes — and the outcomes become the language of the sale.

| Job to Be Done | Functional Need | Emotional Need | Social Need |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Help me grow my business" | A clear, evidence-based growth strategy with prioritised actions and defined success metrics | Confidence that I am making the right calls with the resources I have — and that if it does not work, it was not for lack of rigour | Respect from peers, board members, and investors who see me as a leader with a credible, well-reasoned plan |
| "Make my brand compelling" | A complete brand identity system — positioning, messaging architecture, visual identity, and tone of voice — that reflects our actual quality | Pride in how my organisation shows up in the world; the satisfaction of seeing our brand and thinking "yes, that is exactly who we are" | Credibility and recognition in my market: being seen as a serious, authoritative player by clients, competitors, and talent |
| "Turn data into decisions" | Data infrastructure design, analytics frameworks, and insight processes that connect raw data to strategic and operational decisions on a regular cadence | Certainty and control: the feeling of knowing what is actually happening in my business and being able to act on it with confidence rather than instinct | Authority as a data-led organisation — being known internally and externally as a business that makes smart, evidence-based choices |
| "Get transformation to actually happen" | A sequenced implementation roadmap with clear ownership, milestones, and a delivery partner who is accountable for progress alongside the internal team | Relief: the feeling that someone credible and capable is genuinely owning this alongside me, and that it will not fade away after the first board presentation | Reputation as a leader who does not just commission strategy but delivers change — visible, lasting transformation that colleagues and clients notice |

## Mapping Pain to Services

Every pain point that brings an organisation to Alchemetryx maps directly to a service and a measurable outcome. Use this table to connect the language of the problem to the language of the solution — in discovery calls, proposals, and delivery scoping.

| Pain Point | Alchemetryx Service | Key Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy Paralysis | Growth Strategy & Prioritisation | A clear, board-ready growth strategy with three to five prioritised initiatives, defined metrics, and a 90-day action plan |
| Brand Invisibility | Brand Strategy & Identity | A complete brand system — positioning, messaging, and visual identity — that closes the gap between how the organisation looks and how good it actually is |
| Data Without Direction | Data Strategy & Analytics | A data infrastructure design, measurement framework, and insight cadence that makes data a live strategic input rather than a retrospective report |
| The Execution Gap | Transformation Roadmap & Implementation Support | A sequenced delivery roadmap with milestones, ownership, and embedded accountability checkpoints that keep transformation on track |
| Talent Limitations | Strategic Advisory Retainer | On-demand access to senior strategists, brand experts, and data analysts — without the overhead of hiring, managing, or retaining a full internal team |
| Growth Plateau | Commercial Diagnostic & Growth Strategy | A structured audit of the growth model — brand, channels, ICP, pricing, and data — to diagnose the stall and identify the highest-leverage interventions |

<Tip>
  In discovery calls, listen for Jobs-to-be-Done language rather than waiting for the prospect to name a service. Phrases like "I need clarity on where to focus", "my team is overwhelmed and we keep going in circles", "our brand doesn't match the quality of what we actually do", or "I have all this data and still can't answer the board's questions" are buying signals — not just problems. Each one maps directly to a job Alchemetryx is built to do. Reflect that language back in your proposal and you will write something that feels less like a pitch and more like a diagnosis.
</Tip>
