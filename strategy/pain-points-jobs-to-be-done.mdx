The businesses Alchemetryx serves do not arrive asking for transformation. They arrive with a process that only works because someone remembers how it goes. This page lists the real operational pain points we resolve, the jobs our buyers hire us to do, and the exact entry offer we match to each situation.

We never lead with an abstract concept. We lead with a named process: the rota, the quote, the invoice, the onboarding, the report. That is the operating rule.

## Core operational pains

These are the six pain points that appear most often in discovery calls with owner-led firms of 25–150 staff. Each includes the concrete cost signal the buyer feels, and the Alchemetryx entry offer we match to it.

    **Entry offer:** One Process Rebuild, £5,950 ex VAT. We rebuild the rota so it is visible, owned, and does not depend on one person.

  <Card title="Client onboarding is manual and error-prone" icon="user-plus">
    **Cost signal:** Every new client triggers a different sequence of emails, forms, and handovers. Information is lost. The client experience is uneven. Internal time is buried, not billed.

## Jobs-to-be-done

These statements capture why a buyer hires Alchemetryx, not what we technically deliver. They follow the classic format: when [situation], I want to [action], so I can [outcome].

| When | I want to | So I can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One process only works because I remember how it goes | Hand it over so it runs without me | Stop being the single point of failure |
| My team quotes the same job at three different prices | Build one quoting process everyone follows | Protect margin and speed up response time |
| We bought Copilot licences six months ago | See whether anyone is using them for real work | Decide whether to renew, train, or stop |
| A report that should take an hour takes half a day | Get the numbers direct from source, once | Use that time for work that pays |
| Our Zapier automations break and no one owns them | Know what we have, what works, and what does not | Decide to fix, rebuild, or retire without fear |

## Level 1 vs Level 2 mapping

Our two-level routing splits buyers by where they sit on the AI and automation curve. The pain points differ, and so does the diagnostic framing.

| Level | Situation | Typical pain | Diagnostic framing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 1: AI Adoption** | No deliberate AI or automation. Processes run on spreadsheets, memory, and manual handovers. | The rota breaks when one person is away. Quoting takes days. Reporting is manual. | "Start with one process. AI is what makes it run without you." |
| **Level 2: AI Usage** | Already using Copilot, ChatGPT, Zapier, or prior automation. Cannot show a measured return. | Licences bought, but no one can say what changed. Automations exist but are brittle or orphaned. | "You bought the AI. Did anything change?" |

<Note>
  Level 1 buyers often think they need AI first. They do not. They need one process rebuilt so it is reliable. Only then does AI become useful as the engine that keeps it running. Level 2 buyers already have tools. They need an honest audit of what is working, what is not, and what to do next.
</Note>

## What we will not solve

Not every operational problem is an Alchemetryx problem. We are a Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory, not an IT support desk or a software vendor. We do not take on:

- **Multi-process transformation programmes.** We rebuild one process at a time. If the brief is "fix everything in the business," we refer the prospect to a larger systems integrator.
- **Open-ended advisory with no scoped outcome.** Every engagement defines the process, the deliverable, and the go-live date.
- **Brand-new custom software builds.** We rebuild using existing tools, automations, and straightforward integrations. If the need is a bespoke application, we advise on the requirement and introduce a development partner.
