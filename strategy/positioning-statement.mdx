Alchemetryx is a **Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory**, not an agency, software vendor, or IT support desk. This page states the ratified positioning, what we stand for, and what we deliberately avoid.

## Category definition

Our category descriptor is **Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory**.

We occupy the independent layer between SME technology spend and business outcomes. Most firms in our target segment have bought tools but do not have a system. We make that distinction central to our positioning: tools are not a system, and more software rarely fixes a process that was never designed.

## Positioning statement

For owner-led UK firms of 25–150 staff who have accumulated tools without a system, or who still run critical processes on people and spreadsheets, Alchemetryx is a Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory that audits what is already owned, decides what earns its place, and rebuilds the one process worth rebuilding. Unlike agencies and generalist consultants that sell more tools or vague change programmes, we start with a measured baseline and only recommend action where evidence supports it.

## Position to own

We aim to own three ideas in the mind of the buyer:

1. **Measurement first.** Every engagement starts with a baseline. If we cannot show what changed, we do not propose it.
2. **System design, not tool stacking.** We design how a process works, then select or keep the technology that serves it.
3. **Restraint.** We say no to projects where proof is weak, budgets are too thin to deliver outcomes, or the client is not ready to own the result.

## Do NOT position against

Never claim or imply the following, even in competitive situations:

- Enterprise-grade technology at a fraction of the cost
- Guaranteed ROI or fixed return figures
- Universal AI readiness or one-size-fits-all adoption
- Compliance certifications we do not hold
- Broad vertical expertise without case-study proof

| **Functional benefit** | A process runs without the owner. |
| **Emotional benefit** | Calm. Control. No more single-point-of-failure. |
| **Higher purpose** | Every SME can see how its business runs, and what to change to make it better. |

## Retired positioning

The following descriptors and taglines are retired. Do not use them in any external or internal material:

- "Business Systems, Decision & Growth Intelligence Partner"
- "The Intelligence Operating System for SMEs That Refuse to Stay Small"
- "Transform Data Into Gold"
- "Digital Growth & Intelligence Retainer"
- Fixed ROI claims
- Marketing-analytics three-pillar offer
