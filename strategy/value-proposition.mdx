Alchemetryx is a Business Systems and Intelligence Advisory for owner-led UK firms with 25 to 150 employees. This page maps what those business owners are trying to do, what hurts today, and how the Alchemetryx offer is designed to remove that pain and create a measurable gain, without a six-month engagement. It covers both the full offer architecture and the interim MVP used in the next 30 to 45 days.

## Customer profile

The people we work with are owners, CEOs, and COOs who run the business day-to-day and still touch the same slow process every week: writing the rota, chasing invoices, onboarding a new hire, or compiling a report that should already exist.

    - Get one high-pain process to run without the owner.
    - Know whether tools they already bought, such as Copilot, ChatGPT, or Zapier, actually paid back.
    - Get a clear next step without signing up for a 6-month engagement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pains" icon="alert-triangle">

    - Weak visibility into whether money spent on AI is producing a return.
    - Tool fatigue from buying software that teams barely use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gains" icon="trending-up">

    - A fixed price for a defined scope, not an open-ended estimate.
    - A working system handed over, not a recommendation deck.
    - Published proof from real engagements, not generic case studies.
    - Retainer support for ongoing tuning, plus an honest answer when doing nothing is the best option.
  </Card>

## Fit summary

If you are an owner-led UK firm of 25 to 150 staff and one recurring process still needs you personally to keep it moving, Alchemetryx is designed to remove that bottleneck in a single fixed-price engagement.

- **Level 1 (AI Adoption):** you have no deliberate AI or automation programme. The message is simple: "Start with one process. AI is what makes it run without you."
- **Level 2 (AI Usage):** you already pay for Copilot, ChatGPT, Zapier, or prior automation tools but cannot show a measured return. The question is: "You bought the AI. Did anything change?"

Both paths lead to the same outcome: a working process, a measured baseline, a clear next step, and an honest recommendation.

## Pricing philosophy

Prices are not published on the public website. Each engagement is quoted with a fixed fee for Diagnostic and Build work, and a monthly fee for Retainer support.

If a budget needs adjustment, the scope is changed, not the price. The only exception is the MVP One Process Rebuild floor of £4,950 (ex VAT), which requires founder approval. Negotiation does not mean a discount on the same scope; it means a re-scoped piece of work.
